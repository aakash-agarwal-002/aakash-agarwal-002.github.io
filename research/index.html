About Me

I am an AI researcher and MS by Research graduate in Computer Science from IIT Bombay. My research focuses on LLMs, post-training, machine unlearning, reasoning, multimodal AI, and safety. I am first/equal-contribution author of papers at AAAI 2026 and EMNLP 2025.

My current work, HyperLoRA, is a hypernetwork that generates per-shard LoRA adapters from learned shard embeddings. It enables auditable unlearning through O(1) embedding deletion while keeping the base LLM frozen. On the TOFU benchmark, deployable routing reaches 0.80 AGG, comparable to retraining from scratch (0.81) and well above the strongest tuned post-hoc baseline (0.53).

At Fujitsu Research, I developed Hypothesis-Driven Reasoning (HDR), an iterative generate-and-verify framework for LLM reasoning over multimodal observations, improving accuracy by up to 56.7% over baselines. At CFILT, IIT Bombay, my MS research on depression relapse detection from social media showed that modeling cognitive biases improves accuracy from 0.81 to 0.88.
